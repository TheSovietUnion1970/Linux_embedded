1. ====== Stairs 
1.1. Numbers of steps of stairs -> top if 1 or 2 steps at a time
  => step[n] = step[n - 1] + step[n - 2]
 
1.2. Minumum cost of stairs if 1 or 2 steps at a time
  => min[i] = cost[i] = min(min(cost[i-1]), min(cost[i-2]))
